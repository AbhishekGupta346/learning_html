Story Overview:-

Mirzapur is controlled by Akhandanand Tripathi (Kaleen Bhaiya) brothers Guddu Pandit and Bablu Pandit are his two aides. Who carry all his illegal activities while he feels his son Munna is useless.Munna and Guddu share a grudge as they love the same girl Sweety who prefers Guddu over Munna.In Jaisalmer Babban Babua ditches his partner Bhairav Singh and elopes with a drug consignment and wants to sell it in Mirzapur.Babban's cousin Minsiter JP Yadav a close friend of Kaleen Bhaiya tells him that Mirzapur belongs to Kaleen Bhaiya and no one else.Babban removes all details of Kaleen Bhaiya and goes to Mirzapur to make the deal.But Kaleen Bhaiya does not give him positive response.While in a shop Babban gets in scuffle with Vasudha Pandit mother of Guddu who wants him dead at any cost.Kaleen Bhaiya decides to make the deal with Babban and sends Guddu and Bablu to Jaisalmer to check samples of consignment. Where Guddu realizes Babban was the one who insulted his mother.But before things can get worse they make a deal.Baban insists Guddu to get Kaleen Bhaiya to Jaisalmer to do make the deal as Kaleen Bhaiya can be killed only outside Mirzapur.While Kaleen Bhaiya agrees to go for the deal along with Munna,Maqbool,Bablu and Guddu.Where Munna has planned to kill Guddu.But in between two plans of killing Kaleen Bhaiya and Guddu..A third person is secretly planning assassination of Kaleen Bhaiya.
